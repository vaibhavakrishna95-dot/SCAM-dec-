Why DBSCAN in Scam Detection?

Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise (DBSCAN) groups messages that are closely packed together based on cosine distance in reduced semantic space.

Noise (-1) as Anomaly Detection

Points that do not belong to any dense cluster are classified as Noise (-1). In cybersecurity, this isolates zero-day phishing phrasing, unusual character distributions, or uncoordinated spam attempts.

Dense Clusters as Campaigns

Dense clusters represent coordinated spam campaigns (e.g. repeated lottery formats or bulk credit offers) that share near-identical TF-IDF feature distributions.

Architectural Rule: As specified in the project guidelines, DBSCAN serves as an exploratory clustering and outbreak discovery component; the supervised classifier handles production single-message verdicts.
