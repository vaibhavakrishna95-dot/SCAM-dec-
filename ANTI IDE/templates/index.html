How ScamShield Works

A streamlined pipeline from raw input to verified cybersecurity risk evaluation.

Submit SMS text, an email excerpt, or a suspicious website address. The system standardizes casing, normalizes tokens, and sanitizes characters.

02

Dual Analysis

The text is vectorized into TF-IDF space for ML probability prediction while the heuristic threat rules scan for urgency, OTP demands, and URL deceptions.

A calibrated Risk Level (Low, Medium, High) is calculated along with clear red flags, plain-English explanations, and actionable safety steps.
